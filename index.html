Generative Models · Physics-aware Video Generation · 3D Reasoning · Physical Scene Understanding

I received my MSc in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from University College London. I am currently a visiting student at the University of Cambridge.

My research interests lie in understanding and generating structured representations of the physical world. I am particularly interested in physics-aware video generation, 3D geometric reasoning, dynamic scene representations, and controllable generative models. My broader goal is to develop generative systems that can reason about the underlying geometry, physical properties, and dynamics of the environments they generate.

I am also interested in extending generative modeling techniques to scientific and biomedical applications.

I am seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2027 in Computer Science, particularly in generative models, computer vision, and 3D understanding.
